Hidden Costs
What's not on the invoice, but shows up in the P&L
20% Parts Price Premium
When techs buy at Home Depot or Lowes, Apex Pros pays MSRP instead of Ferguson wholesale. On every self-sourced run, the part itself costs more before you count the tech's time.
$4,160 / yr in markup alone (est.)
Warehouse Throughput Drag
Every warehouse run pulls a person off the Dublin Road or Dayton floor. The BMI system and daily Ferguson pulls don't stop while they're in traffic; the backlog does.
$8,034 / yr in double labor (est.)
Blocked Billable Revenue
NATE-certified techs billing at $200/hr don't generate revenue while sitting in a Home Depot parking lot. That's not a delivery cost; it's an opportunity cost hiding inside a parts run.
$27,300 / yr in blocked billable (est.)
Return Visit Truck Rolls
Industry data shows ~25% of emergency part runs result in a return visit the next day when the wrong part arrives or the job stalls. Each callback costs tech time, mileage, and customer goodwill.
$5,850 / yr in return visits (est.)

What staying in-house costs extra
Apex Pros is currently spending an estimated $65,830/yr in combined labor, parts markups, and blocked billable revenue to run parts 5 times a week with warehouse staff and field techs. Dispatch's ASAP delivery at $78/run ($20,280/yr) keeps NATE-certified technicians billing at the job site, keeps warehouse staff at the Dublin Road and Dayton floors, and eliminates the 20% Home Depot markup entirely. The question isn't whether they can afford Dispatch. It's whether they can afford to keep doing it this way.
